# Limitations and Honest Claims

- A finding is an OBSERVATION from a network scan, not a confirmed vulnerability.
- An open port or a version banner does not prove a weakness or a legal violation.
- Compliance mappings show relevance to a control; they are NOT legal compliance certification or legal advice.
- Mappings marked needs_manual_review require a human to confirm them.
- NIST and CIS control IDs and titles were verified in public sources (see reference-verification.md).
- The Ethiopian mapping (C-007) is NOT verified: the exact article number still needs to be confirmed from the official text.
- The rules only use what a basic TCP scan can observe (port, protocol, service, version).
